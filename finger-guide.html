SPACEBAR

Use your Left or Right Thumb to press Spacebar.

Which Finger Should You Use?

🩷 Left Pinky

Press Q, A, Z and the left-side keys such as Shift.

🧡 Left Ring Finger

Press W, S and X.

💛 Left Middle Finger

Press E, D and C.

💚 Left Index Finger

Press R, T, F, G, V and B.

🩵 Right Index Finger

Press Y, U, H, J, N and M.

💙 Right Middle Finger

Press I, K and comma (,).

💜 Right Ring Finger

Press O, L and full stop (.).

💗 Right Pinky

Press P, semicolon (;), slash (/) and the right-side keys.

⭐ Important Typing Tips

• Keep your fingers resting on A, S, D, F and J, K, L, ;.
• Use both thumbs for the Spacebar.
• Look at the screen instead of the keyboard.
• Focus on accuracy before increasing speed.
• Practise for 10–15 minutes every day.
