About KeySprint

KeySprint is an interactive typing practice website designed to help users improve their typing speed, accuracy, and confidence.

Users can choose different difficulty levels, complete timed typing challenges, view their results, and unlock achievements as they improve.

Our goal is simple: make typing practice engaging, accessible, and rewarding for everyone.
